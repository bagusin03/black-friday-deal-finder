How the prices were checked

Every deal on this page was read on 27 November 2026, Black Friday itself, from a deals source that names the store — CNN Underscored, TechSpot, Popular Science, RedmondPie, Hunt4Freebies, USA Today, FullCleared, or Nintendo's own holiday announcement — and the source is printed on each card. The discount on every card is the difference of the two prices shown. No price here was invented, and no deal is older than this sale.

Verdicts: New low means the lowest price the source reports for that listing. Strong price is a big, well-evidenced cut. Solid deal is a worthwhile cut. Routine sale is a small cut from list price — worth knowing, not a bargain. Where a "was" price is only a list price and the street price was lower, the card says so.
